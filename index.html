Food guide: practical choices

Protein

Foods: eggs, beans, lentils, chickpeas, peas, shiro, milk/yogurt, fish, chicken, lean meat.

Include a protein source at main meals and rotate plant and animal sources as appropriate.

Carbohydrate & energy

Foods: teff injera, whole-grain bread, oats, maize, potatoes, sweet potatoes, rice, kocho.

Choose minimally processed, higher-fibre options where possible. Carbohydrates are not automatically unhealthy.

Vegetables

Foods: cabbage, kale/gomen, carrots, tomatoes, onions, spinach, seasonal vegetables.

When practical, aim to fill about half your plate with vegetables.

Fruit & fibre

Foods: orange, banana, papaya, avocado, seasonal fruit.

Choose whole fruit more often than juice; portion needs vary by individual.

Healthy fats

Foods: groundnuts, sesame, avocado, small amounts of vegetable oil.

Use modest portions and measure added oils if managing weight.

Hydration & balanced meals

Drink safe water regularly. A meal example is injera with shiro or lentils and gomen, or egg with whole-grain bread and vegetables.

Adjust food choices to budget, availability, and health advice.

Health note: BMI is a screening measure and does not distinguish muscle from fat. Calorie and macronutrient estimates can be inaccurate, especially during pregnancy or with illness, kidney/liver disease, diabetes medication, frailty, or a history of disordered eating. Do not use this calculator for children or adolescents. If you have a medical condition or are planning a major weight change, consult a qualified health professional.
